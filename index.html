A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Good start.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No coding experience is needed for the core lessons. The local AI module introduces installation and simple terminal commands with a step-by-step approach.

Do I need paid AI subscriptions?+

Start by exploring the free options available to you. The course covers how to compare free and paid plans. Third-party subscriptions are separate, and tool features and availability can change.

Will Ollama work on my laptop?+

It depends on your operating system, available memory, storage, and the model you choose. The local AI module covers checking compatibility and choosing a model that fits your hardware.

Is local AI completely private?+

Running a model locally can give you more control over processing, but privacy also depends on the software, integrations, network settings, and how you store data. You’ll learn how to check the full setup.
